How verification works

1. Student submits: Hours, supervisor details, or certification information.

2. Trusted source confirms: A supervisor confirms hours; the school or credential issuer confirms training or certification.

3. TradesMatch records the result: The profile shows Pending, Verified, or Needs Review, with the reviewer and confirmation date where appropriate.

Demo limitation: These forms only create local sample requests in this browser. They do not contact supervisors, schools, ASE, or other issuers, and they do not independently verify documents. Do not enter real private information in this prototype.
